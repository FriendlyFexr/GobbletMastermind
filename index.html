    Rules

    Red and Blue each have six pieces: two each of sizes 1, 2 and 3. On your turn you either place a piece from your supply, or move one of your pieces that is on top of a stack. A larger piece may cover a strictly smaller one (yours or the opponent's). You can't skip a turn. Get three of your pieces on top in a row to win.

    If you lift a piece and it uncovers an opponent piece that completes their line, they win (if your move also completes your own line at the same time, they still win). A game is a draw after 60 total moves or if a player has no legal move.

    How to play: click a piece in your supply (or one of your pieces on the board), then click a highlighted square. Pieces underneath a stack are drawn as dashed circles inside the top piece.
